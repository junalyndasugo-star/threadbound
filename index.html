Threadbound

In the city of Auronde, the great looms don't just weave cloth —
they weave logic. Every apprentice learns to code by learning to weave.
You are Wren, newly apprenticed to the Loomkeeper of Auronde. Your first task sits on the frame in front of you: a half-finished pattern, and a stack of instructions that look suspiciously like code. The Loomkeeper says the same words work on both silk and machines — "a pattern is just a set of instructions that don't get tired of repeating themselves."

Six lessons stand between you and your first commission. Let's start at the frame.

Chapter 3 · Repeat the Weave

The Loomkeeper taps the pattern card. "This border calls for five passes of red before the color changes. Watch — I don't retie the thread five times. I tell the loom to repeat itself."

She hands you the frame. The pattern card has one line missing: how many times should the loom repeat the red pass? Fill in the blank and run it.
